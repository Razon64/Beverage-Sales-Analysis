# Retail Beverage Sales Analysis (Excel)

## Project Overview
This repository contains a comprehensive performance analysis of a retail supermarket's beverage department over a 4-week period. Utilizing advanced Microsoft Excel techniques, this project identifies profitability trends, cashier efficiency, and price-sensitivity scenarios.

## Project Objectives
- Profitability Audit: Calculate Gross Margin and GM% across different manufacturers.
- Performance Benchmarking: Identify top-performing brands and product sizes.
- Operational Analysis: Evaluate cashier productivity based on scan volume.
- Predictive Simulation: Use "What-If" analysis to forecast margin growth via Goal Seek.

## Key Insights & Findings
- Margin Leaders: While Coca-Cola and PepsiCo drive the highest volume, specific 500ml and 1L variants yielded 12% higher profit margins than bulk 2L sizes.
- Efficiency Gap: The top 10% of cashiers handle nearly 30% of total scan volume, highlighting a training opportunity for lower-tier staff.
- Price Elasticity: Goal Seek simulations indicated that a 20% discount on Diet Coke (2L) requires a 15% increase in volume to maintain current margin levels.

## Technical Skills Demonstrated
- Data Modeling: Calculated fields for Gross Margin and GM%.
- Lookup Logic: Utilized `XLOOKUP` and `VLOOKUP` for manufacturer categorization.
- Data Summarization: Dynamic Pivot Tables for Brand/Size performance.
- Visualization: Conditional formatting to highlight low-margin "danger zones."
- What-If Analysis: Goal Seek used for strategic pricing simulations.


## Business Impact
This analysis provides stakeholders with a data-backed roadmap to:
1. Optimize Inventory: Prioritize high-margin product sizes.
2. Strategic Discounting: Set minimum volume targets for promotional events.
3. Labor Management: Reward high-efficiency cashiers and target training for others.
